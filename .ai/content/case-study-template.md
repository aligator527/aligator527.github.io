# Engineering Case Study Template

## 00 / Summary

- One-sentence project definition.
- Role, team, timeline, engagement type, visibility status.
- Three to five capabilities demonstrated.

## 01 / Context

- Organization or anonymized domain.
- Existing system and operating environment.
- Why the work mattered.
- Ivan’s exact responsibility and boundaries.

## 02 / Problem

- What was failing, missing, slow, risky, or difficult?
- Who experienced the problem?
- What evidence showed it was real?

## 03 / Constraints

Examples: legacy systems, security, WCS integrations, team skill distribution, delivery date, budget, mobile environments, multilingual use, NDA.

## 04 / Decisions

For each important decision:

1. Decision statement.
2. Alternatives considered.
3. Evaluation criteria.
4. Tradeoff accepted.
5. Consequence observed.

Link an ADR when a public version exists.

## 05 / System

- Architecture overview.
- Data and event flow.
- Security boundaries.
- Important implementation details.
- Technologies connected to responsibilities, not displayed as decoration.

## 06 / Delivery

- Team topology and collaboration.
- Planning, standards, reviews, and release method.
- Risks and how they were managed.

## 07 / Outcome

- Verified quantitative metrics when available.
- Otherwise, precise qualitative outcomes and observable changes.
- Separate personal contribution from team result.

## 08 / Retrospective

- What worked.
- What did not.
- What Ivan would change today and why.
- Remaining limitations.

## Publication checklist

- Every claim is supported.
- NDA and security review complete.
- Diagram has accessible explanation.
- No fake dashboard metrics or reconstructed UI presented as production screenshots.
- The case study reveals reasoning, not only technology names.
