# ADR-0002: Engineering Editorial art direction

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-09-18

## Context

Generic developer portfolios converge on dark themes, neon gradients, terminal motifs, rounded cards, and interchangeable landing-page structures. Ivan’s positioning is broader: full-stack engineering, architecture, technical leadership, enterprise systems, and logistics.

## Decision

Adopt Engineering Editorial: warm paper surfaces, graphite type, vermilion signal color, structured annotations, controlled asymmetry, diagrams, and project-specific compositions.

## Alternatives

- Dark terminal theme: rejected as clichéd and too narrow.
- Generic minimal portfolio: rejected because identity would depend only on copy.
- SaaS landing-page aesthetic: rejected because it frames a person as a product funnel and encourages generic sections.

## Consequences

- Typography, content hierarchy, and diagrams carry more responsibility.
- Custom CSS and careful responsive art direction are required.
- Anti-AI review is a release gate.

## Revisit when

The professional positioning or primary audience changes substantially.
