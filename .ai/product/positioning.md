# Positioning

## Recommended descriptor

**Tech Lead / Full-Stack Engineer**

Supporting line:

> Web systems · architecture · cloud · enterprise software

Core promise:

> I design and ship complex web systems — from requirements and architecture to production.

Treat this as direction, not immutable copy. Any replacement must remain factual, concrete, and concise.

## Differentiators

- Strong React and TypeScript frontend capability paired with backend and AWS delivery.
- Experience across architecture, requirements definition, standardization, review, and release—not implementation alone.
- Enterprise and operational-system context, including WMS, SaaS, workflow systems, and integrations.
- Product-oriented 0→1 delivery experience.
- Professional work across Japanese and international communication contexts.
- Russian, Japanese, and English language capability.

## Narrative hierarchy

1. Systems ownership.
2. Technical leadership and architecture judgment.
3. Full-stack implementation depth.
4. Enterprise and logistics specialization.
5. Multilingual, Tokyo-based, internationally available profile.

## Avoid

- “Passionate developer.”
- “Building digital experiences.”
- “Turning ideas into reality.”
- “10x engineer,” “ninja,” “rockstar,” or “guru.”
- Positioning based on age or exceptionalism.
- Inflated seniority unsupported by the work shown.
