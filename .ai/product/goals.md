# Product Goals

## Primary outcome

Convert qualified international visitors into conversations about high-value freelance, consulting, contract, and engineering-leadership work.

The portfolio must answer within one minute:

1. What kind of engineer is Ivan?
2. Which difficult problems can he own?
3. What evidence supports that claim?
4. How can the visitor continue evaluating him?
5. How can the visitor contact him?

## Positioning outcome

Present Ivan as a product-oriented Full-Stack Engineer and Tech Lead who can take complicated real-world systems from requirements and architecture through implementation and production.

Do not reduce the positioning to “React developer.” React is a strong capability, not the whole identity.

## Experience outcome

The site should feel:

- precise rather than flashy;
- technically deep rather than tool-list driven;
- distinctive rather than template-based;
- concise at the overview level and detailed in case studies;
- globally credible while clearly grounded in Tokyo and Japanese enterprise work.

## Engineering outcome

The implementation itself must demonstrate:

- excellent semantic HTML and CSS;
- restrained client JavaScript;
- accessibility and responsive behavior;
- durable content architecture;
- production-grade testing and deployment;
- explainable architecture decisions.

## Non-goals

- A one-page résumé clone.
- A generic SaaS landing page.
- A “hacker terminal” costume.
- A gallery of every small repository.
- A personal diary or lifestyle influencer site.
- A speculative claim that Ivan has experience he does not have.
