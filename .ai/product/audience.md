# Audiences

## Priority 1 — Engineering decision-makers

Examples: founders, CTOs, VPs of Engineering, engineering managers, staff engineers.

They need to assess technical depth, ownership, communication, architecture judgment, and delivery risk. Lead them to case studies, system decisions, implementation evidence, and GitHub.

## Priority 2 — International clients and consultancies

They need to know what Ivan can own, how he works remotely, whether communication is reliable, and what engagement types fit. Lead them to selected work, capabilities, availability, and contact.

## Priority 3 — Recruiters and hiring managers

They scan quickly for role level, experience, stack, location, languages, work history, and résumé. Provide a clear TL;DR and a downloadable résumé without making the entire site look like a résumé.

## Priority 4 — Technical peers

They evaluate implementation quality, open-source work, experiments, architectural reasoning, and writing. Lead them to Lab, Notes, GitHub, and technical retrospectives.

## Visitor constraints

- Many visitors will spend fewer than 60 seconds initially.
- Some will arrive directly on a project or résumé route.
- Some will use mobile devices or keyboard navigation.
- Some cannot access proprietary code or screenshots.
- English is the primary language; wording must be internationally legible and avoid Japanese corporate jargon without explanation.
