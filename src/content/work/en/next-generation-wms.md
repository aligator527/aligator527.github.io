## 00 / Summary

I joined Frameworx, part of Daiwa House Group, in September 2026 as Tech Lead for a next-generation warehouse management system (WMS). A WMS runs the operational work inside a warehouse — receiving, storage, picking, and shipping. It usually has to exchange instructions and status with a warehouse control system (WCS), which drives automated equipment.

The work is in progress. Product details, architecture, and delivery dates are not public, so this brief states responsibility only.

## 01 / Responsibility

My responsibility covers technical planning and architecture across the frontend, backend, cloud, and security layers, and the integration boundary between the WMS and WCS.

## 02 / Scope

- Technology selection.
- Architecture standardization and engineering guidelines.
- Architecture decisions.
- Technical proofs of concept.
