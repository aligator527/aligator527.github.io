# Content Rules

- Read `.ai/content/facts.md`, `claims.md`, and `voice.md` before authoring public copy.
- Never invent roles, dates, technologies, team sizes, clients, metrics, testimonials, awards, or outcomes.
- Use `[FACT REQUIRED: ...]` when evidence is missing.
- Separate Ivan’s contribution from team outcomes.
- Respect NDA and privacy constraints.
- Prefer concrete decisions and evidence over adjectives.
- Explain Japanese domain or corporate terms for an international audience.
- Keep drafts marked as drafts until content review passes.
